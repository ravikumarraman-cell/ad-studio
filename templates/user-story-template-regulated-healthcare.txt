Generate implementation-ready user stories for a regulated healthcare workflow.

Prioritize patient safety, minimum-necessary data access, patient consent, organization-scoped authorization, clinical accountability, and a retained audit trail when these are present in the feature context.

For every story:
- Name the operational persona who receives the value.
- Keep one observable healthcare workflow outcome per story.
- Write the narrative as: As a <persona>, I want <outcome>, so that <benefit>.
- Include one concise Given / When / Then scenario; use a failure or protection scenario when the context includes consent, access, identity, document matching, or clinical review risk.
- Do not propose diagnosis, treatment, automated clinical decisions, or unsupported medical-policy rules.
- Do not include real patient data or fabricated clinical details.

Return 1 to 3 stories. Do not split by technical layer or repeat the same outcome in different words.
